## Featured work

**[Prince George's Coffee Site Selection](https://github.com/Malay19/prince-georges-coffee-site-selection)** — Site-selection screen over all **214 census tracts** in Prince George's County, MD. Integrated Census ACS, LEHD workplace, and scraped business and review data into a common tract grain, then scored survivors in an interactive Tableau model with adjustable weights.
`Python` `pandas` `Tableau` `Census & LEHD APIs` `sentiment analysis`

**[Yelp Review Usefulness Prediction](https://github.com/Malay19/yelp-review-usefulness-prediction)** — Stacked ensemble over **1.58M labeled reviews** predicting review usefulness under a hard business constraint: false positives at or below 10%. Reached **0.9081 AUC, recovering 69.1% of useful reviews at a 9.70% false-positive rate**. Fourth place in a class-wide competition.
`XGBoost` `LightGBM` `scikit-learn` `TF-IDF` `VADER`

**[Uber Dynamic Pricing Decoded](https://github.com/Malay19/uber-dynamic-pricing-tableau)** — Four linked Tableau dashboards explaining fare structure, surge mechanics, and geographic demand across **99,899 trips**. Built with parameter-driven scenario filters so stakeholders run their own comparisons. Live on Tableau Public.
`Tableau` `geospatial` `scenario modeling`

**[Monte Carlo Marketing Budget Allocation](https://github.com/Malay19/monte-carlo-marketing-budget-allocation)** — Simulated **58 feasible splits** of a $100K monthly budget across three channels under uncertain conversion rates, click costs, and order values — ranking on probability of profit, not just expected value.
`Excel` `@RISK` `Monte Carlo`

**[Multi-Stage Supply Chain Optimization](https://github.com/Malay19/Multi-Stage-Supply-Chain-Optimization)** — Three-stage pipeline chaining store pricing, warehouse allocation, and vehicle routing under real capacity and feasibility constraints. **$58K+ simulated daily profit.**
`AMPL` `Gurobi` `optimization`

**[TransitPulse KPI Analytics](https://github.com/Malay19/TransitPulse-WMATA-KPI-Analytics)** — Relational model across **9+ core tables** joining ridership, reliability, and safety data, with SQL views computing YoY change and crime-per-million-riders to surface multi-factor hotspot stations.
`SQL` `relational modeling` `KPI design`
